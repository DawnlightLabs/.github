## What we work on

- **Smaller, efficient models.** Models that do useful work with far less compute, so they run on a laptop, a phone, your own server, or the cloud at a fraction of the cost. We call it AI independence: you choose where it runs and who controls it.
- **AI security.**
- **Brain-computer interfaces**, starting with EEG.

## Projects

**[Dawnroom](https://dawnroom.dawnlightlabs.com)** is our first open-source project: a local-first image studio. Make and edit images on your own GPU; nothing leaves your machine.
[Website](https://dawnroom.dawnlightlabs.com) · [Repository](https://github.com/DawnlightLabs/dawnroom)

## How we share our work

Open source is a core principle for us. Not everything we make will be open, and we will always say which is which. Some research we publish once it has been reviewed; some stays in the lab.
